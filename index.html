      FAQ

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I pay?

      You can pay by UPI or cash after the service. For bridal bookings, a small advance is taken to confirm the booking.

      How do I cancel or reschedule?

      Message us on WhatsApp at least 4 hours before the service and we will give you a new slot.

      Is a trial available for bridal makeup?

      Yes, a trial session is available for bridal bookings. Chat with us on WhatsApp for details.

      Can I get several services in one visit?

      Absolutely. Select as many services as you like and they will all be done in one appointment.

      What if I have allergies or sensitive skin?

      Tell us in the note box of the booking form. Your beautician will choose products accordingly.
